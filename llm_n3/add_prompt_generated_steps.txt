Ingredients:
1. 12 lasagna noodles (ensure they are vegan-friendly)
2. 1 cup of vegetable broth
3. 1 large eggplant, sliced into thin rounds
4. 1 large zucchini, sliced into thin rounds
5. 1 large bell pepper, sliced
6. 1 can (28 oz.) crushed tomatoes
7. 1 cup cashew cream (blend soaked cashews with water and a little bit of salt)
8. 1/2 cup nutritional yeast
9. Salt, garlic powder, and dried basil to taste

Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to package instructions. Drain and set aside.
3. In a large saucepan, sauté the eggplant, zucchini, and bell pepper until softened. Add crushed tomatoes, salt, garlic powder, and dried basil, then simmer for 10 minutes.
4. In a separate bowl, mix cashew cream and nutritional yeast.
5. In a 9x13-inch baking dish, layer the sauce, lasagna noodles, more sauce, and finally the cashew cream mixture. Repeat these layers until all ingredients are used up, ending with the cashew cream on top.
6. Cover the dish with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 10-15 minutes, or until the top is golden brown.
7. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving. Enjoy your delicious vegan lasagna!
